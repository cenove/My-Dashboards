🎯 Technical & Business Highlights

1. Data Modeling (Star Schema)

<img width="687" height="581" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c0d1f956-32cd-4338-8135-7e2a0131c7e7" />

Robust data structuring, transforming a flat file into a dimensional model:

    Fact Table: Financial transactions.
    Dimension Tables: Calendar (DAX), Products, Segments, and Countries.
    Benefit: Performance optimization and cross-filtering integrity.

2. Visualization & Storytelling

The layout was designed following an executive information hierarchy:

    Layer 1 (KPIs): Macro view of Sales, Profit, and Margin with goal comparisons (Goal vs. Actual).
    Layer 2 (Deep Dive): Use of conditional formatting to highlight the product "Paseo" (Sales Leader) and Scatter Plot analysis to identify high-profitability outliers.
    Layer 3 (Trends): Temporal chart utilizing native intelligence features.

3. Analytics & Forecasting

Leveraging Power BI's native AI engine for scenario projection:

    Application of Forecasting algorithms (Exponential Smoothing) to project sales for the upcoming semester (Jan/2015 onwards).
    Confidence interval configured to identify volatility and expected deviations.

🛠️ Tools Used

    Power Query: ETL and data cleaning.
    DAX: Development of calculated measures for Margin %, Goal Variance, and Time Intelligence.

    Power BI Desktop: Visualization and publishing.
